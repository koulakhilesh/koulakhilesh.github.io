These posts start from a register of 56 churches linked to Christopher Wren, built from an encyclopedia list and checked against Historic England listings, church websites and a catalogue his son published in 1750. Every date and number carries a label for how well it is supported.

The first post looks at the rebuilding after 1666 and the slower losses that followed. The second follows the stone, bells and parishes that moved, then turns what the survivors publish about visiting into a yes/no flow chart for choosing one.
