These posts start with a map of London's 65 Saturday parkruns: about 4.7 million of the city's 8.8 million people live within 2 km of one, measured in a straight line.

From there, I follow the data into smaller questions: which event came first, what the names share, which one is loneliest, whether an alphabet route is possible, and where five new events might reach the most people.
