On walks, I try to guess who London's blue plaques commemorate. I scraped English Heritage's 1,037 plaques to look at the list as a whole; the people remembered form a smaller, more uneven portrait of London than I expected.

The companion piece maps their clustering, draws nearest-plaque territories across the city, and finds a route through the boroughs.
